# OpenCBT — Project Blueprint

## Vision
An open-source Community Based Tourism (CBT) platform that lets local communities in Bangladesh — starting with Sylhet — earn directly from responsible tourism, while preserving culture and heritage.

## Mission
Connect local communities, tourists, and researchers on one lightweight digital ecosystem, so tourism income flows directly to the people who host it.

## Problem Statement
- Local communities (e.g. Khasia/Khasi in Jaflong) rarely capture direct income from tourism; middlemen absorb most value.
- Tourists lack a trustworthy way to discover and book authentic, community-run experiences.
- No open dataset or public research layer exists to study CBT impact in Bangladesh.

## What is CBT?
Community Based Tourism (CBT) is tourism managed and benefited from by the local community itself — homestays, guided experiences, cultural sharing — rather than external operators.

## Tourism Impact Model
Community → Experience → Tourist → Direct Income → Heritage Preservation & Local Development

## Community Empowerment Strategy
- Communities control their own profile, pricing, and content.
- No middleman booking fees beyond a minimal platform sustainability fee.
- Heritage archive gives cultural documentation value beyond tourism income.

## Target Users
1. **Local Community** — families, farmers, artisans, cultural performers, guides
2. **Tourists** — responsible travelers seeking authentic experiences
3. **Researchers** — studying CBT impact, sustainability, heritage
4. **Platform Admin** — verification, moderation, analytics

## Technology Strategy
Lightweight, low-bandwidth-friendly stack: Next.js + TypeScript + Tailwind + Shadcn UI frontend; Next.js API or Supabase backend; PostgreSQL (Supabase); Supabase Auth & Storage; OpenStreetMap/Leaflet for maps; deployed on Vercel/Cloudflare.

## Pilot: Sylhet, Bangladesh
- **Jaflong** — nature tourism, river landscape, community experience
- **Khasia (Khasi) Community** — indigenous culture, betel leaf tradition, heritage knowledge
- **Sylhet Region** — tea gardens, rural life, traditional food, crafts

## Future Expansion
Bandarban, Sundarbans, Haor regions, other rural Bangladesh destinations.
